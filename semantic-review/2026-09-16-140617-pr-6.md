```text
protected candidate
        |
all production-credentialed hosts in maintenance
        |
custom domain selects exact candidate
        |
        +-- exact 006 ----------> 007 -> 008 -> 009 -> 010
        |
        `-- missing/partial 006 -> 006 -> 007 -> 008 -> 009 -> 010

```

The unknown old application remains eligible only before new security/catalog boundaries. After missing 006 or migration 008 is reached, the pinned new release is the application recovery target; later states require the corresponding RPC and payment contracts. A migration failure therefore leaves traffic blocked at the last compatible matrix state, while database restoration remains a separately controlled recovery operation requiring commerce reconciliation.

Same-project promotion preserves domain/DNS ownership and avoids the fallback transfer’s detach/claim window. The fallback does not become eligible merely because `fasteno-shyama` exists: ownership evidence, atomic reassignment or confirmed immediate claimability, destination protection before detachment, and tested rollback remain prerequisites.
